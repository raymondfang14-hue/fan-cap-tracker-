How are the PlainStats power rankings made?

Each team gets a score from 0 to 100: 28% win percentage, 28% scoring margin, 24% roster strength (the PlainStats player ratings) and 20% efficiency (offense and defense EPA per play, sacks and turnovers), each as a rank among the 32 teams. No opinions or votes go in.
