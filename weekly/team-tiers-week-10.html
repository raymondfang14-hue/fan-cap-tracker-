Quick answers

Who is the best team in the NFL after week 10?

By net EPA per play (offense EPA minus defense EPA allowed), the Jacksonville Jaguars at +0.232. The best offense belongs to the San Francisco 49ers and the best defense to the Minnesota Vikings.

Which team rose the most in week 10?

The Detroit Lions, up 6 spots to 8th.

Which team fell the most in week 10?

The New England Patriots, down 6 spots to 21st.

How are the team tiers built?

Every team is ranked by net EPA per play: how many points its offense adds per pass or run play, minus how many its defense allows. The 32 teams are then grouped into five tiers. For a defense, a lower EPA allowed is better.
