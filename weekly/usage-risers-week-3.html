Quick answers

Who had the biggest target share jump in week 3?

Brock Bowers (TE, Raiders): 43% of his team's targets (13 of 30), up from – in his earlier games, a change of +43 pts.

Who gained the most carries in week 3?

Ollie Gordon II (RB, Dolphins) took 55% of his team's carries (17 of 31), up from 6% before.

Whose role shrank the most in week 3?

Justin Jefferson (WR, Vikings) fell to 8% of the targets from 36% over his earlier games (−28 pts).

What is target share?

A player's targets divided by all of his team's targets in that game. Carry share is the same with carries (quarterback runs count in the team total), and snap share is his share of the offense's snaps. "Before" is his share over his earlier games this season, so a jump means a bigger role than he has had.
