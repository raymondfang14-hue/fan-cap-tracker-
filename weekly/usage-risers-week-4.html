Quick answers

Who had the biggest target share jump in week 4?

Darius Cooper (WR, Eagles): 28% of his team's targets (7 of 25), up from 4% in his earlier games, a change of +24 pts.

Who gained the most carries in week 4?

Braelon Allen (RB, Jets) took 100% of his team's carries (14 of 14), up from 22% before.

Whose role shrank the most in week 4?

Ja'Marr Chase (WR, Bengals) fell to 6% of the targets from 24% over his earlier games (−19 pts).

What is target share?

A player's targets divided by all of his team's targets in that game. Carry share is the same with carries (quarterback runs count in the team total), and snap share is his share of the offense's snaps. "Before" is his share over his earlier games this season, so a jump means a bigger role than he has had.
