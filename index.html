Fifth-year option decisions

The option adds a fully guaranteed 2028 season. Its price follows the CBA, roughly: the top-10 average salary at the position for picks 1 to 10, and the average of the 3rd to 25th highest for picks 11 to 32, before Pro Bowl bumps. About

About PlainStats

PlainStats explains NFL contracts and the salary cap for everyday fans. Every deal shows what is really guaranteed, what the player counts against the cap each year, and what happens if his team cuts or trades him. Team pages, player ratings, the draft board and the front-office tools all build on the same numbers.

Contract figures come from named news reports and team announcements, plus PlainStats' own calculations from the rookie pay scale and the collective bargaining agreement. Each player shows where his numbers come from, and a contract with no confirmed figure yet shows "Contract being verified" instead of a dollar amount. Player stats come from nflverse. Ratings, grades, estimates and award odds are PlainStats' own models built from public data. They are estimates, not official figures, and they update as new stats and reports arrive.

Found a wrong number or have an idea? Use or email info@plainstatsnfl.com. A contract figure changes once a named report confirms it.

Site setup

For whoever runs the site. Contract and cap figures live in data/nfl.json, the news feed in data/moves.json and season stats in data/stats.json (built by data/build_stats.py from nflverse files). A daily task refreshes all three each morning.

The Draft page reads data/draft.json, built by data/build_draft.py from public big boards and the college stats in data/draft_stats.txt (from cfbstats). Update the ranks and stats there, rerun the script, and the board, grades, mock and rookie pay all recompute in the page.

Airtable holds the featured deal breakdowns. Set AIRTABLE_TOKEN and AIRTABLE_BASE_ID (and AIRTABLE_TABLE if the table is not named Contracts) in Vercel, and the page reads them through /api/contracts. Without them, the built-in breakdowns show.

Working Copy keeps the code. Edit src.html in Koder, run build.sh or copy it into index.html, then commit and push. Vercel redeploys on its own.

Ads stay off until you add a Google AdSense publisher ID to ADS.client and your live domain to ADS.hosts near the end of the script. Until then the slots are hidden.
