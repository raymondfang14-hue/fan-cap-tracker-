Quick answers

How much cap space do the Panthers have?

The Carolina Panthers have about $920K of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 32nd most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Panthers have?

The Panthers carry about $26.8M in dead money this season, 20th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Panthers head coach?

Dave Canales is the Panthers head coach, in the job since 2024.
