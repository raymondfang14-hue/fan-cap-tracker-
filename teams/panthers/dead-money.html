What is dead money in the NFL?

Dead money is cap space used by players a team cut or traded. It is mostly bonus money that had not been counted yet when the player left.

Why do the Panthers have dead money?

Dead money builds up when a team releases or trades a player before his contract ends. The Panthers still owe bonus money and guaranteed salary tied to the players listed here.
