Quick answers

How much cap space do the Cardinals have?

The Arizona Cardinals have about $11.4M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-11-09, 12th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Cardinals have?

The Cardinals carry about $91.6M in dead money this season, 5th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Cardinals head coach?

Mike LaFleur is the Cardinals head coach, in the job since 2026.
