Quick answers

How much cap space do the Commanders have?

The Washington Commanders have about $24.5M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 6th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Commanders have?

The Commanders carry about $26.7M in dead money this season, 21st most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Commanders head coach?

Dan Quinn is the Commanders head coach, in the job since 2024.
