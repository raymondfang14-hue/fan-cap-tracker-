Quick answers

How much cap space do the Raiders have?

The Las Vegas Raiders have about $7.6M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 21st most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Raiders have?

The Raiders carry about $58.4M in dead money this season, 9th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.
