Quick answers

How much cap space do the Patriots have?

The New England Patriots have about $4.4M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 27th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Patriots have?

The Patriots carry about $44.1M in dead money this season, 16th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Patriots head coach?

Mike Vrabel is the Patriots head coach, in the job since 2025.
