Quick answers

How much cap space do the Seahawks have?

The Seattle Seahawks have about $12.6M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 11th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Seahawks have?

The Seahawks carry about $4.2M in dead money this season, 32nd most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Seahawks head coach?

Mike Macdonald is the Seahawks head coach, in the job since 2024.
