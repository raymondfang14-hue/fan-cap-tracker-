Quick answers

How much cap space do the Chiefs have?

The Kansas City Chiefs have about $2.2M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 29th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Chiefs have?

The Chiefs carry about $15.6M in dead money this season, 27th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Chiefs head coach?

Andy Reid is the Chiefs head coach, in the job since 2013.
