Quick answers

How much cap space do the Vikings have?

The Minnesota Vikings have about $6.1M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 24th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Vikings have?

The Vikings carry about $55.2M in dead money this season, 10th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Vikings head coach?

Kevin O'Connell is the Vikings head coach, in the job since 2022.
