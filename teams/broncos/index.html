Quick answers

How much cap space do the Broncos have?

The Denver Broncos have about $4.7M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 26th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Broncos have?

The Broncos carry about $7.6M in dead money this season, 31st most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Broncos head coach?

Sean Payton is the Broncos head coach, in the job since 2023.
