Quick answers

How much cap space do the 49ers have?

The San Francisco 49ers have about $41.3M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 1st most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the 49ers have?

The 49ers carry about $41.9M in dead money this season, 17th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the 49ers head coach?

Kyle Shanahan is the 49ers head coach, in the job since 2017.
