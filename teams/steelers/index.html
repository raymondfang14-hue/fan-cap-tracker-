Quick answers

How much cap space do the Steelers have?

The Pittsburgh Steelers have about $11.1M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 13th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Steelers have?

The Steelers carry about $21.6M in dead money this season, 23rd most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Steelers head coach?

Mike McCarthy is the Steelers head coach, in the job since 2026.
