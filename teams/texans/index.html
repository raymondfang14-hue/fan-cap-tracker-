Quick answers

How much cap space do the Texans have?

The Houston Texans have about $9.0M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 16th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Texans have?

The Texans carry about $73.3M in dead money this season, 7th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Texans head coach?

DeMeco Ryans is the Texans head coach, in the job since 2023.
