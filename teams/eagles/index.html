Quick answers

How much cap space do the Eagles have?

The Philadelphia Eagles have about $8.2M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 18th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Eagles have?

The Eagles carry about $82.1M in dead money this season, 6th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Eagles head coach?

Nick Sirianni is the Eagles head coach, in the job since 2021.
