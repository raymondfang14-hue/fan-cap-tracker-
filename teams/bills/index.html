Quick answers

How much cap space do the Bills have?

The Buffalo Bills have about $3.8M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-11-11, 28th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Bills have?

The Bills carry about $49.0M in dead money this season, 11th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Bills head coach?

Joe Brady is the Bills head coach, in the job since 2026.
