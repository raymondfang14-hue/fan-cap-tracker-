Quick answers

How much cap space do the Rams have?

The Los Angeles Rams have about $990K of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 31st most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Rams have?

The Rams carry about $13.6M in dead money this season, 28th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Rams head coach?

Sean McVay is the Rams head coach, in the job since 2017.
