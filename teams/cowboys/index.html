Quick answers

How much cap space do the Cowboys have?

The Dallas Cowboys have about $16.2M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 8th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Cowboys have?

The Cowboys carry about $47.3M in dead money this season, 14th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.
