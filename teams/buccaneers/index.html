Quick answers

How much cap space do the Buccaneers have?

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers have about $15.2M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 9th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Buccaneers have?

The Buccaneers carry about $16.6M in dead money this season, 26th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Buccaneers head coach?

Todd Bowles is the Buccaneers head coach, in the job since 2022.
