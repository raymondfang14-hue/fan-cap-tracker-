Quick answers

How much cap space do the Colts have?

The Indianapolis Colts have about $19.6M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 7th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Colts have?

The Colts carry about $20.5M in dead money this season, 24th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Shane Steichen is the Colts head coach, in the job since 2023.
