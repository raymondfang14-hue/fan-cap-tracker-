Quick answers

How much cap space do the Packers have?

The Green Bay Packers have about $7.8M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 20th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Packers have?

The Packers carry about $48.6M in dead money this season, 12th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Packers head coach?

Matt LaFleur is the Packers head coach, in the job since 2019.
