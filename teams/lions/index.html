Quick answers

How much cap space do the Lions have?

The Detroit Lions have about $35.2M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 2nd most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Lions have?

The Lions carry about $34.1M in dead money this season, 19th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Lions head coach?

Dan Campbell is the Lions head coach, in the job since 2021.
