Quick answers

How much cap space do the Jaguars have?

The Jacksonville Jaguars have about $4.8M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 25th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Jaguars have?

The Jaguars carry about $61.4M in dead money this season, 8th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Liam Coen is the Jaguars head coach, in the job since 2025.
