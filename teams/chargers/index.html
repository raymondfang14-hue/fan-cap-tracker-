Quick answers

How much cap space do the Chargers have?

The Los Angeles Chargers have about $26.0M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 5th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Chargers have?

The Chargers carry about $9.1M in dead money this season, 30th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Chargers head coach?

Jim Harbaugh is the Chargers head coach, in the job since 2024.
