What is the 2026 NFL salary cap?

The 2026 NFL salary cap is $301.2M per team.

What is cap space?

Cap space is the room a team has left under the salary cap after counting every contract on its books, including dead money from players who already left.

How much of the Bengals cap is dead money?

About $12.2M of the Bengals' cap is dead money, which is 4.0% of the league cap.
