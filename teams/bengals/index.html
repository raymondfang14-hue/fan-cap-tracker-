Quick answers

How much cap space do the Bengals have?

The Cincinnati Bengals have about $8.7M of 2026 cap space as of 2026-10-22, 17th most among the 32 teams. The league cap is $301.2M.

How much dead money do the Bengals have?

The Bengals carry about $12.2M in dead money this season, 29th most in the NFL. Dead money is cap space used by players a team already cut or traded.

Who is the Bengals head coach?

Zac Taylor is the Bengals head coach, in the job since 2019.
