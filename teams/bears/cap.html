Quick answers

How much cap space do the Bears have?

The Chicago Bears have about $9.5M of cap space for the 2026 season, 15th most among the 32 NFL teams, as of 2026-11-11.

What is the 2026 NFL salary cap?

The 2026 NFL salary cap is $301.2M per team.

What is cap space?

Cap space is the room a team has left under the salary cap after counting every contract on its books, including dead money from players who already left.

How much of the Bears cap is dead money?

About $25.3M of the Bears' cap is dead money, which is 8.4% of the league cap.
